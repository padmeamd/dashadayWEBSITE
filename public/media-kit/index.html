About me

I'm Dasha Day, an international digital creator, filmmaker and independent musician with a passion for cinematic storytelling and creating content that leaves a lasting impression.

I'm currently creating in London, with previous work filmed for brands and businesses across Dubai, Los Angeles and Moscow, bringing together cinematic visuals, authentic storytelling and a distinctive creative perspective.

My work spans travel, lifestyle, luxury hospitality, fashion, entertainment and music. From boutique hotels and extraordinary destinations to restaurants, immersive experiences and cultural venues, I specialise in transforming places, products and experiences into captivating visual stories that inspire audiences to explore, discover and engage.

Over the years I've collaborated with brands and venues including Skittles UK, Mr Fogg's, Prangsta and Bunga 90, creating engaging, story-driven content tailored to both the brand's identity and its audience.

Alongside my work as a digital creator, I'm an independent recording artist with over 2.7 million music streams worldwide, listeners in 170+ countries, and a combined audience of 100,000+ followers across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Spotify.

I'm also the creator of Things I Shouldn't Say, an original AI-powered cinematic mystery series inspired by my music, combining filmmaking, storytelling and emerging technology to bring an entirely fictional universe to life.

With a background in filmmaking, technology and digital media, I bring both artistic vision and a strategic approach to every collaboration. Whether I'm capturing the atmosphere of a historic hotel, showcasing a unique experience or bringing a creative concept to life, my goal is always the same: to make people feel something.

I don't just create content. I create stories people want to watch.

Working together

What a collaboration can look like

  • Integration inside the story

    A product or location written into an episode of the series rather than bolted onto the end of a video — period-appropriate or contemporary, depending on the strand.

  • An original track

    Music composed for the campaign and released under my name, so the sound keeps working long after the flight ends.

  • Reels and short form

    Vertical pieces in the voice the audience already follows, scored with my own music and shot in London or on location.

  • Events and coverage

    Hotel openings, restaurant launches, premieres and fashion weeks — London and the UK first, and further afield on request. I attend as press and film the piece myself.

  • Talks on AI filmmaking

    How a software engineer builds a cinematic series alone: the pipeline, the tooling, the craft. For technology brands and conferences.
